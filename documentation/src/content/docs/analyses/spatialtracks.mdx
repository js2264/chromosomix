**Spatial tracks** turns a 3D model into tracks on the map's bins — radial position, distance to a locus and local compaction — to read beside the map, colour the model by, or give to any 1D task.

## Running it

Open a contact map and put a model on the **Structures** card; until then the task is greyed on the **3D tasks** card of the **Analysis** panel. Choose the model under **structure**. For the distance track, give a locus under **distance to**: type it, or press **pick on map** and <kbd>Shift</kbd>-drag over the stretch on the map. Left empty, only the other two tracks are made.

The tracks are lanes beside the map, rows of the **Tracks** card in the **Annotations** panel, marked `*` and named after the model: **Radial position**, **Local compaction** with its window, as in `Local compaction (±5 beads)`, and **Distance to the centre of** the locus. A phased model has tracks for each haplotype.

## Parameters

A small **compaction window** follows the chain bead by bead; a wider one averages a longer stretch. **distance to** takes a point or a window, measured from the centre of the beads it covers.

## Reading the result

Every value is a distance in the model's own units, a root-mean-square radius of 1 for a model made by [Model from the map](/analyses/reconstruct3d/), so the tracks compare positions along one model, not between models. Each bin takes the mean over the beads whose middles fall in it; a bin no bead falls in is a gap.

- **Radial position**: the distance from the centre of mass of every bead the model and the map share, which on a model of one chromosome is that chromosome's own centre. High is far out.
- **Distance to the centre of** a locus, the spatial virtual 4C: low is close. Set it beside the map's [4C profile](/analyses/fourc/) from the same anchor: a model that honours the map puts near the anchor what the map counted often with it. With no bead at the locus, the track is not made, and a note says so.
- **Local compaction**: each bead's mean distance to its neighbours along the chain, within the window. Small is compact; a bead with no neighbour on its sequence has no value.

To colour the model by a track, choose it under **colour by** on the **3D structure** card.
