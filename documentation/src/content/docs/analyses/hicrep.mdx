**Reproducibility** scores every pair of maps on screen with HiCRep's stratum-adjusted correlation coefficient, the SCC. A plain correlation of two maps is near 0.9 even for unrelated ones, because every map falls with distance; HiCRep smooths both maps and correlates each diagonal on its own. Use it on replicates before merging them, or to put a number on how far two conditions are apart.

## Running it

Put the maps side by side in one deck at the same resolution: press **+** along the edge of the map and drop another file in ([Maps side by side](/browse/deck/)). Then choose **Reproducibility** in the **2D tasks** card of the **Analysis** panel (<kbd>4</kbd>) and press **run**. Every pair of the deck's maps is compared; with one map, the row waits.

A map at another resolution is left out and named, and a run with no other map on its grid is refused; an `.mcool` opens at any resolution it stores. A sequence whose length differs between two maps is left out, with a note.

The drawer shows **Stratum-adjusted correlation**, the matrix of every pair on a fixed 0 to 1 scale; **Pooled over every sequence**, every pair, highest first; **Per sequence**, every pair on every sequence, with the strata each pooled; and **Correlation by separation**, a line per pair.

![A deck of three chicken maps of chromosome 7 at 100 kb, DT40 cells in G2 and 5 and 60 minutes into mitosis, beside the Reproducibility drawer: the coefficient matrix over 36 sequences, G2 against 5 min at 0.62 and 60 min against G2 and 5 min at 0.15 and 0.22, the pooled and per-sequence tables, and the correlation by separation with a line per pair.](../../../assets/screens/analyses/hicrep-deck-drawer.webp)

The same run from a shell, with each other map named after `--against`:

<TaskCommand id="hicrep" />

```sh
chromosomix hicrep rep1.mcool::/resolutions/100000 --against rep2.mcool::/resolutions/100000 \
  --against rep3.mcool::/resolutions/100000 --out results/
```

## Parameters

Both settings, like the resolution, change the answer: compare coefficients made with the same settings at the same resolution. **furthest separation compared** is how many strata count, as hicrep's `dBPMax`: 5 Mb is 50 strata at 100 kb. **smoothing half-width** is the mean filter applied to both maps first; at 0, two shallow replicates can score worse than two deep maps of different conditions.

## Reading the result

An SCC is a correlation, at most 1; two maps that agree in structure score near it. The pooled coefficient weights each stratum of each sequence by the positions it compares, so a long chromosome counts for more than a short one. In **Per sequence**, one sequence far below the others is worth opening on the deck.

**Correlation by separation** says more than the number. Two maps that agree near the diagonal and part further from it differ in depth; two that disagree at every separation are two experiments. Where a stratum compares nothing, the line has a gap, not a zero.

### What to watch for

- **Balancing.** In the app a file with weights is read balanced, while HiCRep is defined on raw counts: for hicrep's own number, run it with `--no-balance` on the command line.
- **On a deck**, every map's run computes the same pairs, so the **all maps** tab shows the comparison once.

## Compared with hicrep

The arithmetic is that of `hicrep` 0.2.6, the Python port of the R package: in [the validation](https://chromosomix.app/validation/#reproducibility-hicrep), on raw counts, every pair agrees with it to the four decimals written.
