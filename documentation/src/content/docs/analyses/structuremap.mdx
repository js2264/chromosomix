Beads close together in a 3D model should have been caught together often on the map. **Model versus map** sets the model's distances against the map's contacts, as pictures and as one number per sequence, so you can judge a model before reading anything off it.

## Running it

Open a contact map and put a model on the **Structures** card: a g3d, `.3dg` or bed3d file, a PDB that chromosomix wrote ([3D models](/files/structures/)), or one from [Model from the map](/analyses/reconstruct3d/). Then, on the **3D tasks** card of the **Analysis** panel, greyed until both are there, choose the model under **structure** and the **sequences**.

The model must name its sequences as the map does, or the run stops and says so. Each bin takes the mean position of the beads whose middles fall in it, so the model need not be at the map's resolution; a bin no bead falls in is left out.

## Parameters

**sequences** decides which sequence the pictures are of, the first one named; the table covers every sequence the model and the map share. **exponent α** changes only the predicted-contacts picture, since every power of `1/d` ranks the pairs alike: use the exponent the model was made with, 3 for a model made here at the default.

## Reading the result

### The agreement

The table gives, for each sequence, and each haplotype on a phased model, **Spearman ρ (1/d vs contacts)**: the rank correlation between the inverse of the model's distance and the observed contacts, over every pair of modelled bins at least two bins apart. 1 is a model that orders every pair as the map does; near 0, its distances say nothing about the contacts. **Bins modelled** and **Pairs compared** say how much of the sequence that number is about.

Being a rank, ρ ignores the exponent and the model's scale. It is not the ρ at the end of Model from the map's table, which is over every pair the map counted, across sequences.

### The pictures

- **Model distances**: the model's distance between every two bins of the sequence, in the map's frame.
- **Predicted contacts**: the contact map those distances predict, `1/d^α`, up to scale. A faithful model predicts the map's domains and far contacts.
- **Model distance by separation**: the model's mean distance at each separation, on log–log axes, then its slope, beside **The map's P(s) over the same bins**. Where the map's contacts fall away with separation, the model's distances should rise.

To compare one model with another, use **compare with** on the **3D structure** card, which gives the RMSD between the two.
