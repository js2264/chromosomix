# Code of conduct

chromosomix follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in its issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

In short: be welcoming and considerate, criticise work rather than people, and accept that a
decision may not go your way. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing anyone's private
information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code privately to the maintainer, Jacques Serizay, at
<jacquesserizay@gmail.com>. Every report is read, answered and kept confidential. The maintainer
may edit or remove contributions, and restrict or ban participants, as the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe.
